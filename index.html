Aizu Weekend

Three days by shinkansen and rental car from Koriyama: basashi, a giant wooden Kannon and a sake shop with tastings in Bange, an izakaya night in Aizuwakamatsu, an onsen hotel up in the Minamiaizu highlands, and Ouchi-juku on the way home. Mon 23 is Labour Thanksgiving Day, so expect holiday crowds. Times are a rough guide, not a fixed schedule. The fixed points are the trains, the last hotel check-in (18:00 on Sunday) and the sunset around 16:25.

Before you go

Saturday 21 Nov

Koriyama to Bange to Aizuwakamatsu

Night: Aizuwakamatsu
Open Saturday's route in Google Maps →
  1. 11:00

    Arrive Koriyama, pick up car

    Shinkansen from Tokyo is about 1 hr 20 min. Collect the rental near the station.

  2. About 50 min to Inawashiro on the Ban-etsu Expressway

  3. ~12:15

    Lunch at Lake Inawashiro 猪苗代

    • Wakaki Shokudo 若喜食堂: famous for a huge sauce katsudon. Near Inawashiro Station. Open for lunch on Saturdays (usually closed Sundays). Gets full at noon: 0242-62-3324.
    • Inawashiro Ji-Beer brewhouse 猪苗代地ビール館: five craft beers. Open daytime every day (listings give 11:00 to 17:00 or 9:20 to 17:50). Passengers only. 0242-63-2177.
    • Quick look at the lake with Mt. Bandai behind. Swans start arriving in November.
    • Backup: Michi-no-eki Inawashiro roadside station (katsudon, soft-serve, local produce), 9:00 to 18:00.

  4. About 35 min to Tachiki Kannon in Aizubange, back on the Ban-etsu Expressway past Aizuwakamatsu

  5. ~13:45

    Tachiki Kannon 立木観音 (恵隆寺)

    An 8.5 m thousand-armed Kannon carved from a single standing tree. Hug the pillar in the hall for luck. 9:00 to 16:00, ¥300. Allow about 30 min.

  6. About 7 min into central Bange

  7. ~14:30

    Central Aizubange 会津坂下

    Said to be the birthplace of Aizu-style basashi. These are all a few minutes apart.

    • Goi Saketen 会津旨酒 五ノ井酒店: the sake stop. A liquor shop known for the best range of Bange's three breweries (Toyokuni, Akebono, and Hiroki's local label Izumikawa), and reviews say they let you taste. Open daily, from 9:00 to at least 18:30. Hiroki itself is rarely in stock. 0242-83-2170.
    • Shokudo Ishiyama 食堂いしやまSnack: where Bange's chilled ramen (hiyashi ramen) started, near Goi Saketen. Served year-round, if you have room after lunch. 11:00 to 15:00, then from 17:00. No fixed closing day.
    • Bange Drive-In 堀ドライブイン: cheap basashi on Route 49, with a butcher's shop next door. One listing gives 11:00 to 21:00, closed only on New Year's Day. Call to confirm: 0242-83-3027.
    • Aizu Takasagoya 会津高砂屋: local soy sauce and miso maker, good for souvenirs. 8:10 to 17:00, closed Sundays. One listing says it also closes on the 2nd and 4th Saturdays, but the 21st is the 3rd.

  8. About 5 min east

  9. ~15:30

    Aizu no Beko no Chichi Ice Bokujo 会津のべこの乳 アイス牧場

    The local dairy's own soft-serve shop. Usually 10:00 to 17:00, possibly shorter in winter. 0242-83-2324.

  10. ~15:45

    Michi-no-eki Aizu Yugawa-Aizubange 道の駅あいづ 湯川・会津坂下Optional

    Roadside station on Route 49, a few minutes further on and on the way to Aizuwakamatsu. Lots of Aizu sake and local food to take home. 9:00 to 18:00.

  11. About 20 min to Aizuwakamatsu

  12. ~16:15

    Check in: Aizuwakamatsu Washington Hotel 会津若松ワシントンホテル

    View the Trip.com listing

    Near Aizuwakamatsu Station, about 20 min walk to Sakaemachi, the izakaya area. Park the car for the night. Japan's drink-driving limit is effectively zero.

  13. Evening

    Izakaya hopping in Sakaemachi 栄町

    About 20 min walk (or a short taxi) south from the hotel. Ordered so the earliest deadlines come first. Most of these close on Sundays, so Saturday is the night. Book Kagota.

    1. 17:00
      Bangeya 居酒屋ばんげや · Sakaemachi 1-38Thick-cut Bange basashi and sakura liver sashimi. The ¥2,000 drinks set (drink + main + side) only runs until 20:00, so start here. Open to 22:30.
    2. ~18:45
      Kagota 籠太 · Sakaemachi 8-49Aizu home cooking and local sake. Food last order 21:00, closes 22:00, so this has to be second. Popular: reserve on 0242-32-5380.
    3. ~20:30
      Aizukko 彩喰彩酒 會津っこ · Sakaemachi 4-49Aizu sake and Aizu jidori chicken karaage. The official site gives 17:00 to 22:00, last order 21:30, so arrive by about 20:30. 0242-32-6232.
    4. ~22:00
      the bar cozy · Sakaemachi 8-19Late bar near Kagota, 19:00 to 4:00, irregular closing days. Easy last stop. 0242-33-0752.
    5. Alt.
      Mugitoro 麦とろ · Sakaemachi 4-9Old-style sake bar run by a friendly owner who brings out free extras (homemade umeboshi, fried manju). Basashi with spicy miso and fried tororo nori rolls. 18:00 to 23:30, closes on irregular days. Swap in for Aizukko, or go here after.
    6. Alt.
      Aburaya あ舞らや · Kamimachi 1-6Bar running since 1984, open 19:00 to about 1am, closed Sundays. Kamimachi is just north of Sakaemachi, so it's on your way back toward the hotel.
Sunday 22 Nov

Aizuwakamatsu, then the highlands

Night: Hoshinosato Hotel
Open Sunday's route in Google Maps →
  1. 8:30

    Tsuruga Castle 鶴ヶ城

    Open 8:30 to 17:00 every day, so be there at opening. Castle museum and the view from the top floor. Combined ticket with Rinkaku is about ¥520. Allow about 1.5 hrs. Free parking around the grounds fills later in the day.

    • Rinkaku tea room 麟閣, in the castle grounds. Matcha and a wagashi for about ¥500 extra.
  2. 10:15

    Iimoriyama 飯盛山

    About 10 min east. Done in the morning now, so the sunset closing (about 16:25) is no longer a worry.

    • Sazae-do さざえ堂: wooden spiral pagoda where the up and down paths never cross. ¥400.
    • Byakkotai graves 白虎隊十九士の墓, at the top of the hill. Stairs, or a ¥250 escalator (9:00 to 16:00 from Nov 21).
  3. 11:45

    Lunch: Tsuruga Aizu Main Store 鶴我 会津本店

    About 10 min from Iimoriyama. Horse meat from an Aizu horse farm: red-meat sashimi, sakura nabe, eight-cut platter. Lunch 11:30 to 14:30 (last order 14:00), closed Wednesdays. It moved to Ekimae-machi 6-12, about 5 min walk from Aizuwakamatsu Station. Old listings still show the previous address, so follow the map link, not an old pin. 0242-29-4829.

  4. 13:00

    Nanokamachi street 七日町通り

    About 6 min from the station area. Park once for both this and Suehiro, a short walk away. Old storehouses and Taisho-era shopfronts with lacquerware, Aizu cotton and sweets. Good place to buy souvenirs and persimmons.

  5. 14:00

    Suehiro Sake Brewery 末廣酒造 嘉永蔵

    Free tour, about 30 min, roughly on the hour from 10:00 to 16:00 (none at noon), then tasting. The tour is in Japanese. No booking needed for individuals. Closed on the 2nd Wednesday of the month. 0242-27-0002. The café serves coffee made with the brewing water and sake cake. Tasting is for passengers only: the driver has a mountain drive next.

  6. ~14:45

    About 1 hr 40 min on the main roads: Route 121 south to Aizu-Tajima, then Route 289 west. Leave around 14:45 to arrive near sunset (about 16:25). Last check-in is 18:00.

    Faster option, about 1 hr 20 min: Route 401 through the Hakase Tunnel and Showa village. The last stretch near the hotel is a mountain section that closes for winter (from Dec 1 last year; this year's date wasn't announced as of Oct 22). Only take it if the road is dry and it's still light. Google Maps picks this route by default, so add Aizu-Tajima Station as a stop to keep to the main roads.

  7. ~16:25

    Aizu Kogen Hoshinosato Hotel 会津高原 星の郷ホテル

    Minamiaizu, right by the Nango ski area. Booked with dinner: an Aizu-style French course using local vegetables, meat and fermented foods. Buffet breakfast. Alkaline open-air bath, and stargazing if the night is clear. Check-in 15:00 to 18:00, check-out by 10:00. 0241-73-2121.

Monday 23 Nov · Public holiday

Ouchi-juku, then the long way home

Train: ~17:00 from Koriyama
Open Monday's route in Google Maps →
  1. 8:30

    Check out after breakfast

    Skip a big breakfast if you want room for soba.

  2. About 1 hr 10 min north on Routes 289 and 121, passing Tou-no-Hetsuri on the way. Go straight to Ouchi-juku: its car parks get busy from about 10:30. Tou-no-Hetsuri is where the road home starts, so it fits in on the way out.

  3. ~9:45

    Ouchi-juku 大内宿Go early

    Edo-era post town lined with thatched roofs. Walk to the shrine steps at the far end for the classic view down the street.

    • Misawaya 三澤屋: negi soba (takato soba, about ¥1,320), eaten with a whole leek instead of chopsticks. Open 9:30 to 16:00, closed Wednesdays (open on holidays). Write your name on the list as soon as you arrive. One review says they take reservations up to the day before: 0241-68-2927.
    • Street snacks: tochi-mochi and shingoro (rice grilled with sweet miso).
    • The village is open every day and free to walk. Most shops open around 9:00. Parking is ¥500 per car, cash only.
  4. ~11:15

    About 5 min down the hill

  5. ~11:20

    Yunokami Onsen Station 湯野上温泉駅

    A train station with a thatched roof and an irori hearth in the waiting room. The free foot bath next to it is open all day (cleaning on Wednesdays only), so bring a small towel. 10 to 15 min.

  6. ~11:45

    Tou-no-Hetsuri 塔のへつり

    Eroded rock towers over the river, about 15 min on from the station. A short walk over the suspension bridge, 30 to 45 min. The bridge usually closes for winter once snow arrives (late November to early December). If it's shut, you can still see the towers from the car park side.

  7. ~12:20

    About 40 min east on Route 118, up to the Hatori highland (about 1,000 m, so bring a warm layer). If you'd rather skip British Hills, stay on Route 118 for Michi-no-eki Toki no Sato Ten'ei instead (lunch 11:00 to 15:00, last order 14:30; only 8 min extra on the direct route).

  8. ~13:00

    British Hills ブリティッシュヒルズOptional

    A full medieval English village on the highland, with a manor house, guest houses and shops. Day visitors are welcome. Call ahead to check day-visit conditions: 0248-85-1313 (9:30 to 18:00, every day).

    • Falstaff Pub: fish and chips and other pub food. Lunch 11:30 to 15:00, last order 14:30.
    • Ascot Tea Room: afternoon tea and home-style British food. Days and menu by phone.
    • Leave by about 14:00 to fit in the Archive. Later than that, drive straight to Koriyama (1 hr 7 min).
  9. ~14:00

    About 40 min down Route 118 to Sukagawa

  10. ~14:40

    Sukagawa Tokusatsu Archive Center 須賀川特撮アーカイブセンターOptional

    Free museum of the miniatures, models and props used in Godzilla and Ultraman films. Sukagawa is the hometown of Eiji Tsuburaya, who created Ultraman. 9:00 to 17:00 (last entry 16:45), closed Tuesdays and Wednesdays, so it's open on the holiday Monday. Allow about 30 min. 0248-94-5200.

  11. ~15:10

    About 35 min to Koriyama. Refuel before returning the car.

  12. ~15:45

    Return car, shinkansen to Tokyo at ~17:00

    Souvenir hall on the station's 1F concourse: Mamador and Exon Pie (both from Sanmangoku, a Koriyama confectioner). If you skip both detours you'll reach Koriyama around 14:00, with time for Koriyama Black (a dark soy ramen) near the station.

Notes

  • Weather. Late November in Aizu is cold and can bring the first snow. Pack warm layers.
  • Holiday weekend. Mon 23 is a public holiday. Ouchi-juku, the expressway home and the return shinkansen will all be busier than usual.
  • Driving. Plan the car around drinking. Saturday night is a walk-everywhere night in Aizuwakamatsu. Sunday ends with a 1.5 hr mountain drive, so the driver skips the Suehiro tasting.
  • Hiroki. One of the hardest sake labels in Japan to buy. Drinking it at an Aizu bar is the easier route.

大内宿から寄り道しながら郡山へ

帰り：郡山発 17:00頃
月曜のルートをGoogleマップで開く →
  1. 8:30

    朝食後にチェックアウト

    そばを食べるなら朝食は控えめに。

  2. 国道289号と121号を北へ約1時間10分で大内宿。途中で塔のへつりの近くを通るが、大内宿の駐車場は10:30ごろから混むので先に大内宿へ。塔のへつりは帰り道の入口にあるので、帰りに寄る。

  3. 9:45頃

    大内宿早めに

    茅葺き屋根が並ぶ江戸時代の宿場町。突き当たりの石段を上った高台から通りを見下ろすのが定番の景色。

    • 三澤屋：ネギ1本を箸代わりにして食べる高遠そば（約1,320円）。9:30〜16:00、水曜定休（祝日は営業）。着いたらすぐ名前を書いておく。前日まで予約できるという口コミあり：0241-68-2927。
    • 食べ歩き：栃餅としんごろう（甘い味噌を塗って焼いたごはん）。
    • 集落は年中無休で散策無料。お店は9時ごろから。駐車場は普通車500円（現金のみ）。
