Aizu Weekend

Three days by shinkansen and rental car: sake breweries in Bange, an izakaya night in Aizuwakamatsu, an onsen ryokan in the gorge, and Ouchi-juku on the way home. Mon 23 is Labour Thanksgiving Day, so expect holiday crowds.

Before you go

Saturday 21 Nov

Fukushima to Bange to Aizuwakamatsu

Night: Aizuwakamatsu
  1. 11:00

    Arrive Fukushima, pick up car

    Shinkansen from Tokyo is about 1.5 hrs. Collect the rental at the station.

  2. About 1 hr to Inawashiro on the Ban-etsu expressway

  3. ~12:15

    Lunch at Lake Inawashiro 猪苗代

    • Wakaki Shokudo 若喜食堂: famous for a huge sauce katsudon. Near Inawashiro Station.
    • Inawashiro Ji-Beer brewhouse 猪苗代地ビール館: five craft beers, open 11:00 to 17:00. Passengers only.
    • Quick look at the lake with Mt. Bandai behind. Swans start arriving in November.
    • Backup: Michi-no-eki Inawashiro roadside station (katsudon, soft-serve, local produce), 9:00 to 18:00.

  4. About 40 min to Aizubange

  5. ~13:45

    Aizubange 会津坂下

    Said to be the birthplace of Aizu-style basashi.

    • Hiroki Sake Brewery Honten 廣木酒造本店. Hiroki is very hard to buy, so don't count on bottles.
    • Bange Drive-In 堀ドライブイン: cheap basashi on Route 49. Hours aren't online, so call ahead: 0242-83-3027.
    • Aizu Beko no Gyu ice cream
    • Tachiki Kannon 立木観音 (恵隆寺): an 8.5 m thousand-armed Kannon carved from a single standing tree. Hug the pillar in the hall for luck.
    • Akebono Sake Brewery 曙酒造: a second Bange brewery if Hiroki has nothing to sell. Check whether the shop is open.
    • Shokudo Ishiyama 食堂いしやまSnack: where Bange's chilled ramen (hiyashi ramen) started. Served year-round, if you have room after lunch.
    • Aizu Takasagoya 会津高砂屋: local soy sauce and miso maker, good for souvenirs.

  6. About 20 min to Aizuwakamatsu

  7. ~16:30

    Check in: Aizuwakamatsu Washington Hotel 会津若松ワシントンホテル

    View the Trip.com listing

    Near Aizuwakamatsu Station, about 20 min walk to Sakaemachi, the izakaya area. Park the car for the night. Japan's drink-driving limit is effectively zero.

  8. Evening

    Izakaya hopping in Sakaemachi 栄町

    About 20 min walk (or a short taxi) south from the hotel. Ordered so the earliest deadlines come first. Most of these close on Sundays, so Saturday is the night. Book Kagota.

    1. 17:00
      Bangeya 居酒屋ばんげや · Sakaemachi 1-38Thick-cut Bange basashi and sakura liver sashimi. The ¥2,000 drinks set (drink + main + side) only runs until 20:00, so start here. Open to 22:30.
    2. ~18:45
      Kagota 籠太 · Sakaemachi 8-49Aizu home cooking and local sake. Food last order 21:00, closes 22:00, so this has to be second. Popular: reserve on 0242-32-5380.
    3. ~20:30
      Aizukko 彩喰彩酒 會津っこ · Sakaemachi 4-49Aizu sake and Aizu jidori chicken karaage. Open from 17:00 to 22:00 or 23:00 (listings differ), so check last order before you go: 0242-32-6232.
    4. ~22:00
      the bar cozy · Sakaemachi 8-19Late bar near Kagota, open until 27:00 (3am) on Saturdays. Easy last stop.
    5. Alt.
      Mugitoro 麦とろ · Sakaemachi 4-9Old-style sake bar run by a friendly owner who brings out free extras (homemade umeboshi, fried manju). Basashi with spicy miso and fried tororo nori rolls. 18:00 to 23:30, closes on irregular days. Swap in for Aizukko, or go here after.
    6. Alt.
      Aburaya あ舞らや · Kamimachi 1-6Bar running since 1984, open 19:00 to 25:00 (1am). Kamimachi is just north of Sakaemachi, so it's on your way back toward the hotel.

    Not in the route: 酒菜 天味 (I couldn't confirm where it is; one guide puts it about 10 min by car from the station) and Mokkiri もっきり (Babamachi 4-45, between the hotel and Sakaemachi, but it may only open weekdays). Check both on Google Maps if you want to add them.

Sunday 22 Nov

Aizuwakamatsu, then the onsen

Night: Marumine Kanko Hotel
  1. 7:00

    Kitakata morning ramen 喜多方 朝ラーOptional

    About 30 min north. Soy broth, flat curly noodles. Back in Aizuwakamatsu by about 9:00. Skip it if Saturday night runs late.

    • Bannai Shokudo 坂内食堂: opens 7:00. The classic, known for niku soba (ramen covered in chashu).
    • Makoto Shokudo まこと食堂: opens 7:30. Can have a queue.
  2. 9:00

    Tsuruga Castle 鶴ヶ城

    Open 8:30 to 17:00 every day. Castle museum and the view from the top floor. Combined ticket with Rinkaku is about ¥520. Allow 1.5 to 2 hrs. Free parking around the grounds fills later in the day.

    • Rinkaku tea room 麟閣, in the castle grounds. Matcha and a wagashi for about ¥500 extra.
  3. 11:30

    Lunch: Tsuruga Aizu Main Store 鶴我 会津本店

    Horse meat from an Aizu horse farm: red-meat sashimi, sakura nabe, eight-cut platter. Lunch 11:30 to 14:30 (last order 14:00), closed Mondays. One listing gives the address as Ekimae-machi 6-12, near the station, but another says it moved, so check the address before driving: 0242-29-4829.

  4. 13:00

    Iimoriyama 飯盛山

    About 10 min east. Go in the early afternoon because Sazae-do closes at sunset (about 16:20 in late November).

    • Sazae-do さざえ堂: wooden spiral pagoda where the up and down paths never cross. ¥400.
    • Byakkotai graves 白虎隊十九士の墓, at the top of the hill. Stairs, or a ¥250 escalator (9:00 to 16:00 from Nov 21).
  5. 14:30

    Suehiro Sake Brewery 末廣酒造 嘉永蔵

    Free tour, about 30 min, roughly hourly from 10:00 to 16:00, then tasting. The tour is in Japanese. Sources disagree on whether you need to book, so call ahead: 0242-27-0002. The café serves coffee made with the brewing water and sake cake. Tasting is for passengers only.

  6. 15:30

    Nanokamachi street 七日町通り

    A short walk from Suehiro, so park once for both. Old storehouses and Taisho-era shopfronts with lacquerware, Aizu cotton and sweets. Good place to buy souvenirs and persimmons.

  7. About 25 min south into the gorge. Leave by about 16:30 to arrive before dark.

  8. ~17:00

    Marumine Kanko Hotel 丸峰観光ホテル

    Ashinomaki Onsen 芦ノ牧温泉. Riverside baths. Check-in from 15:00, check-out by 10:00. The main building has served a buffet dinner since 2024, while the Kawaoto annex does kaiseki, so check which one your booking includes.

Monday 23 Nov · Public holiday

Ouchi-juku, then home

Train: ~17:00 from Fukushima
  1. 8:45

    Check out after breakfast

    Skip a big breakfast if you want room for soba.

  2. About 25 min to Ouchi-juku. Get there before the holiday crowds and tour buses.

  3. 9:15

    Ouchi-juku 大内宿Go early

    Edo-era post town lined with thatched roofs. Walk to the shrine steps at the far end for the classic view down the street.

    • Misawaya 三澤屋: negi soba (takato soba, about ¥1,320), eaten with a whole leek instead of chopsticks. Opens 9:30, closed Thursdays. Write your name on the list as soon as you arrive. One review says they take reservations up to the day before: 0241-68-2927.
    • Street snacks: tochi-mochi and shingoro (rice grilled with sweet miso).
  4. 11:15

    Tou-no-Hetsuri 塔のへつり

    Eroded rock towers over the river, about 15 min from Ouchi-juku. A short walk over the suspension bridge, 30 to 45 min.

  5. 12:00

    About 2 hrs to Fukushima. Leave room for holiday traffic, and refuel before returning the car.

  6. ~14:00

    Enban gyoza 円盤餃子If time

    Gyoza fried in a round pan and served as one big disc. Both are close to Fukushima Station. Only worth it if you reach Fukushima by about 14:00, since both stop lunch early or sell out.

    • Manpuku 満腹 · Naka-machi 1-24: the original. Weekends and holidays from 11:40 until sold out.
    • Gyoza Terui East Exit 餃子 照井 東口店 · Sakae-machi 1-1: lunch until 14:15 last order.
  7. ~16:00

    Return car, shinkansen to Tokyo at ~17:00

    Pick up Mamador and other souvenirs at the station.

Notes

  • Weather. Late November in Aizu is cold and can bring the first snow. Pack warm layers.
  • Holiday weekend. Mon 23 is a public holiday. Ouchi-juku, the expressway home and the return shinkansen will all be busier than usual.
  • Driving. Plan the car around drinking. Saturday night is a walk-everywhere night in Aizuwakamatsu.
  • Hiroki. One of the hardest sake labels in Japan to buy. Drinking it at an Aizu bar is the easier route.

福島から坂下、会津若松へ

宿泊：会津若松

    会津の馬刺し発祥の地といわれる町。

    • 堀ドライブイン：国道49号沿いの安くておいしい馬刺し。営業時間がネットに載っていないので事前に電話：0242-83-3027。

    • 立木観音（恵隆寺）：一本の立木から彫られた高さ8.5mの千手観音。お堂の柱を抱くとご利益があるとか。

    ホテルから南へ徒歩約20分（タクシーならすぐ）。ラストオーダーが早い順に並べています。日曜休みの店が多いので、土曜の夜が勝負。籠太は予約を。

    2. 18:45頃

    3. 20:30頃

    6. 候補

宿泊：丸峰観光ホテル

メモ

  • 天気：11月下旬の会津は寒く、初雪が降ることも。暖かい服装で。
  • 連休：23日は祝日。大内宿、帰りの高速道路、新幹線はいつもより混む。
  • 運転：お酒を飲む予定に合わせて運転担当を決めておく。土曜の夜は会津若松を歩いて回る。
  • 飛露喜：日本でも指折りの手に入りにくい銘柄。買うより会津の居酒屋で飲むほうが早い。
