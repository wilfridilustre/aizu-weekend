Aizu Weekend

Three days by shinkansen and rental car from Koriyama: sake breweries in Bange, an izakaya night in Aizuwakamatsu, an onsen hotel up in the Minamiaizu highlands, and Ouchi-juku on the way home. Mon 23 is Labour Thanksgiving Day, so expect holiday crowds. Times are a rough guide, not a fixed schedule. The fixed points are the trains, the last hotel check-in (18:00 on Sunday) and the sunset around 16:20.

Before you go

Saturday 21 Nov

Koriyama to Bange to Aizuwakamatsu

Night: Aizuwakamatsu
Open Saturday's route in Google Maps →
  1. 11:00

    Arrive Koriyama, pick up car

    Shinkansen from Tokyo is about 1 hr 20 min. Collect the rental near the station.

  2. About 50 min to Inawashiro on the Ban-etsu Expressway

  3. ~12:15

    Lunch at Lake Inawashiro 猪苗代

    • Wakaki Shokudo 若喜食堂: famous for a huge sauce katsudon. Near Inawashiro Station.
    • Inawashiro Ji-Beer brewhouse 猪苗代地ビール館: five craft beers, open 11:00 to 17:00. Passengers only.
    • Quick look at the lake with Mt. Bandai behind. Swans start arriving in November.
    • Backup: Michi-no-eki Inawashiro roadside station (katsudon, soft-serve, local produce), 9:00 to 18:00.

  4. About 30 min to Aizubange, back on the Ban-etsu Expressway past Aizuwakamatsu

  5. ~13:45

    Aizubange 会津坂下

    Said to be the birthplace of Aizu-style basashi.

    • Hiroki Sake Brewery Honten 廣木酒造本店. Hiroki is very hard to buy, so don't count on bottles.
    • Bange Drive-In 堀ドライブイン: cheap basashi on Route 49. Hours aren't online, so call ahead: 0242-83-3027.
    • Aizu Beko no Gyu ice cream
    • Tachiki Kannon 立木観音 (恵隆寺): an 8.5 m thousand-armed Kannon carved from a single standing tree. Hug the pillar in the hall for luck.
    • Akebono Sake Brewery 曙酒造: a second Bange brewery if Hiroki has nothing to sell. Check whether the shop is open.
    • Shokudo Ishiyama 食堂いしやまSnack: where Bange's chilled ramen (hiyashi ramen) started. Served year-round, if you have room after lunch.
    • Aizu Takasagoya 会津高砂屋: local soy sauce and miso maker, good for souvenirs.

  6. About 20 min back east to Aizuwakamatsu

  7. ~16:30

    Check in: Aizuwakamatsu Washington Hotel 会津若松ワシントンホテル

    View the Trip.com listing

    Near Aizuwakamatsu Station, about 20 min walk to Sakaemachi, the izakaya area. Park the car for the night. Japan's drink-driving limit is effectively zero.

  8. Evening

    Izakaya hopping in Sakaemachi 栄町

    About 20 min walk (or a short taxi) south from the hotel. Ordered so the earliest deadlines come first. Most of these close on Sundays, so Saturday is the night. Book Kagota.

    1. 17:00
      Bangeya 居酒屋ばんげや · Sakaemachi 1-38Thick-cut Bange basashi and sakura liver sashimi. The ¥2,000 drinks set (drink + main + side) only runs until 20:00, so start here. Open to 22:30.
    2. ~18:45
      Kagota 籠太 · Sakaemachi 8-49Aizu home cooking and local sake. Food last order 21:00, closes 22:00, so this has to be second. Popular: reserve on 0242-32-5380.
    3. ~20:30
      Aizukko 彩喰彩酒 會津っこ · Sakaemachi 4-49Aizu sake and Aizu jidori chicken karaage. Open from 17:00 to 22:00 or 23:00 (listings differ), so check last order before you go: 0242-32-6232.
    4. ~22:00
      the bar cozy · Sakaemachi 8-19Late bar near Kagota, open until 27:00 (3am) on Saturdays. Easy last stop.
    5. Alt.
      Mugitoro 麦とろ · Sakaemachi 4-9Old-style sake bar run by a friendly owner who brings out free extras (homemade umeboshi, fried manju). Basashi with spicy miso and fried tororo nori rolls. 18:00 to 23:30, closes on irregular days. Swap in for Aizukko, or go here after.
    6. Alt.
      Aburaya あ舞らや · Kamimachi 1-6Bar running since 1984, open 19:00 to 25:00 (1am). Kamimachi is just north of Sakaemachi, so it's on your way back toward the hotel.

    Not in the route: 酒菜 天味 (I couldn't confirm where it is; one guide puts it about 10 min by car from the station) and Mokkiri もっきり (Babamachi 4-45, between the hotel and Sakaemachi, but it may only open weekdays). Check both on Google Maps if you want to add them.

Sunday 22 Nov

Aizuwakamatsu, then the highlands

Night: Hoshinosato Hotel
Open Sunday's route in Google Maps →
  1. 8:30

    Tsuruga Castle 鶴ヶ城

    Open 8:30 to 17:00 every day, so be there at opening. Castle museum and the view from the top floor. Combined ticket with Rinkaku is about ¥520. Allow about 1.5 hrs. Free parking around the grounds fills later in the day.

    • Rinkaku tea room 麟閣, in the castle grounds. Matcha and a wagashi for about ¥500 extra.
  2. 10:15

    Iimoriyama 飯盛山

    About 10 min east. Done in the morning now, so the sunset closing (about 16:20) is no longer a worry.

    • Sazae-do さざえ堂: wooden spiral pagoda where the up and down paths never cross. ¥400.
    • Byakkotai graves 白虎隊十九士の墓, at the top of the hill. Stairs, or a ¥250 escalator (9:00 to 16:00 from Nov 21).
  3. 11:45

    Lunch: Tsuruga Aizu Main Store 鶴我 会津本店

    About 10 min from Iimoriyama. Horse meat from an Aizu horse farm: red-meat sashimi, sakura nabe, eight-cut platter. Lunch 11:30 to 14:30 (last order 14:00), closed Mondays. One listing gives the address as Ekimae-machi 6-12, near the station, but another says it moved, so check the address before driving: 0242-29-4829. The map link uses Aizuwakamatsu Station for this stop until the address is confirmed.

  4. 13:15

    Nanokamachi street 七日町通り

    About 6 min from the station area. Park once for both this and Suehiro, a short walk away. Old storehouses and Taisho-era shopfronts with lacquerware, Aizu cotton and sweets. Good place to buy souvenirs and persimmons.

  5. 14:30

    Suehiro Sake Brewery 末廣酒造 嘉永蔵

    Free tour, about 30 min, roughly hourly from 10:00 to 16:00, then tasting. The tour is in Japanese. Sources disagree on whether you need to book, so call ahead: 0242-27-0002. The café serves coffee made with the brewing water and sake cake. Tasting is for passengers only: the driver has a mountain drive next.

  6. 15:15

    About 1 hr 20 min on Route 401, through the new Hakase Tunnel (opened 2023, open all winter) and Showa village. Leave by about 15:15. It gets dark around 16:20, so the last part is a mountain road at dusk. If it's snowing, take the main roads instead: Route 121 south to Aizu-Tajima, then Route 289 west (about 1 hr 40 min). Last check-in is 18:00.

  7. ~16:35

    Aizu Kogen Hoshinosato Hotel 会津高原 星の郷ホテル

    Minamiaizu, right by the Nango ski area. Booked with dinner: an Aizu-style French course using local vegetables, meat and fermented foods. Buffet breakfast. Alkaline open-air bath, and stargazing if the night is clear. Check-in 15:00 to 18:00, check-out by 10:00. 0241-73-2121.

Monday 23 Nov · Public holiday

Ouchi-juku, then home

Train: ~17:00 from Koriyama
Open Monday's route in Google Maps →
  1. 8:30

    Check out after breakfast

    Skip a big breakfast if you want room for soba.

  2. About 1 hr 10 min north on Routes 289 and 121, passing Tou-no-Hetsuri on the way. Go straight to Ouchi-juku: its car parks get busy from about 10:30. Tou-no-Hetsuri is where the road home starts, so it fits in on the way out.

  3. ~9:45

    Ouchi-juku 大内宿Go early

    Edo-era post town lined with thatched roofs. Walk to the shrine steps at the far end for the classic view down the street.

    • Misawaya 三澤屋: negi soba (takato soba, about ¥1,320), eaten with a whole leek instead of chopsticks. Open 9:30 to 16:00, closed Wednesdays (open on holidays). Write your name on the list as soon as you arrive. One review says they take reservations up to the day before: 0241-68-2927.
    • Street snacks: tochi-mochi and shingoro (rice grilled with sweet miso).
    • The village is open every day and free to walk. Most shops open around 9:00. Parking is ¥500 per car, cash only.
  4. 11:45

    Tou-no-Hetsuri 塔のへつり

    Eroded rock towers over the river, about 20 min from Ouchi-juku. A short walk over the suspension bridge, 30 to 45 min.

  5. 12:30

    About 1.5 hrs east to Koriyama on Route 118, through Ten'ei. Leave room for holiday traffic, and refuel before returning the car.

  6. ~14:00

    Koriyama: late lunch and souvenirsFlexible

    About 3 hrs of slack before the train, which covers holiday traffic. If the roads are clear, have a late lunch near the station (Koriyama Black, a dark soy ramen, is the local dish) and shop at the souvenir hall on the station's 1F concourse.

  7. ~16:00

    Return car, shinkansen to Tokyo at ~17:00

    Mamador and Exon Pie (both from Sanmangoku, a Koriyama confectioner) are sold in the station souvenir hall.

Eat & drink list

Notes

  • Weather. Late November in Aizu is cold and can bring the first snow. Pack warm layers.
  • Holiday weekend. Mon 23 is a public holiday. Ouchi-juku, the expressway home and the return shinkansen will all be busier than usual.
  • Driving. Plan the car around drinking. Saturday night is a walk-everywhere night in Aizuwakamatsu. Sunday ends with a 1.5 hr mountain drive, so the driver skips the Suehiro tasting.
  • Hiroki. One of the hardest sake labels in Japan to buy. Drinking it at an Aizu bar is the easier route.

会津の週末

郡山から新幹線とレンタカーで巡る3日間。坂下の酒蔵、会津若松の居酒屋はしご、南会津の高原の温泉ホテル、そして帰りに大内宿。23日は勤労感謝の日なので、どこも混雑に注意。時間はあくまで目安。動かせないのは新幹線、日曜のホテルのチェックイン（18:00まで）、16:20ごろの日没だけ。

会津若松を観光して高原へ

宿泊：星の郷ホテル

    鶴ヶ城

    • さざえ堂：上りと下りの人がすれ違わない木造のらせん堂。400円。
    • 白虎隊十九士の墓：山の上。階段か、有料のスロープコンベア（250円、11/21から9:00〜16:00）。

    ランチ：鶴我 会津本店

    飯盛山から車で約10分。会津の牧場から届く馬肉。赤身刺し、桜鍋、桜八品盛り。ランチ11:30〜14:30（LO 14:00）、月曜定休。住所は駅前町6-12（駅の近く）という情報と、移転したという情報があるので、行く前に確認：0242-29-4829。住所が確定するまで、地図リンクではこの場所を会津若松駅にしています。

    七日町通り

    駅周辺から車で約6分。末廣まで歩いてすぐなので、車は一度停めれば両方回れる。蔵や大正時代の店構えが並び、漆器、会津木綿、お菓子のお店がある。お土産や柿を買うならここ。

  5. 14:30

    会津高原 星の郷ホテル

    南会津町、南郷スキー場のすぐそば。夕食付きで予約済み：地元の野菜や肉、会津の発酵食品を使った会津風フレンチのコース。朝食はビュッフェ。アルカリ性の露天風呂があり、晴れた夜は星空も。チェックイン15:00〜18:00、チェックアウト10:00。0241-73-2121。

メモ

  • 天気：11月下旬の会津は寒く、初雪が降ることも。暖かい服装で。
  • 連休：23日は祝日。大内宿、帰りの高速道路、新幹線はいつもより混む。
  • 運転：お酒を飲む予定に合わせて運転担当を決めておく。土曜の夜は会津若松を歩いて回る。日曜の最後は約1時間半の山道なので、運転する人は末廣の試飲をがまん。
  • 飛露喜：日本でも指折りの手に入りにくい銘柄。買うより会津の居酒屋で飲むほうが早い。
